Map thumbnails are operator-facing pictures for the Matchup and Map series
scenes (and the map cards behind them). The nine active-duty maps ship
pre-filled from the cs2-map-icons "thumbs" pack
(https://github.com/MurkyYT/cs2-map-icons, images/thumbs), resized from
1920x1080 to 1280x720 for the broadcast canvas.

Drop <map>.png here to replace one, or upload a picture per map from the
admin panel: Match setup → map picture (stored under public/uploads/maps/).
An uploaded picture always wins over the shipped thumbnail.

These are scene pictures only. The custom radar uses its own images under
public/radars/ — see public/radars/README.txt and README.md,
"Custom radar images".
